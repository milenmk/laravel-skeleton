## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our project management platform (the "Service"). We are committed to ensuring the privacy and security of your information.

## Data We Collect

### Required Data

- **First and Last Name**: For user identification.
- **Email Address**: For account creation, communication, and collaboration.

### Optional Data

- **Phone Number**: For enhanced communication.
- **Address (Country, City, Street, etc.)**: For personalized features and contact purposes.

### Automatically Collected Data

- Log data (e.g., IP address, browser type, access times).
- Cookies for improving user experience (see our Cookie Policy).

## Purpose of Data Collection

We collect data for the following purposes:

- **Account Management**: To create and manage user accounts.
- **Collaboration**: To enable project managers and team members to collaborate effectively.
- **Communication**: To notify users about project updates or respond to inquiries.
- **Platform Improvements**: To enhance the functionality and security of our Service.

## User Data Control

### Profile Visibility

- Users can hide their profiles or specific information (e.g., phone number, address).
- Users may opt out of being visible to other users, except for invitations via email.

### Data Access and Deletion

- Users can request access to their data or deletion of their accounts by contacting [support email].
- Deleted accounts will remove all associated personal data unless required for legal or operational purposes.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data for profit. Data is shared only when necessary:

- **With authorized project managers**: For collaboration purposes.
- **With third-party services**: For features like social login (e.g., Google, GitHub) in compliance with their respective privacy policies.

## Data Security

We implement the following measures to secure your data:

- Encryption of sensitive data (e.g., passwords).
- Regular security audits and monitoring.
- Limited access to user data by authorized personnel only.

## Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data based on:

- **Consent**: For optional data and features.
- **Contractual Necessity**: To provide the Service.
- **Legitimate Interests**: To ensure platform functionality and security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes via email or platform notifications.
