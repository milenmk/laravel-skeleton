## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly,
provide insights into how the platform is used, and enable additional features like saving your preferences.

---

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. Examples include:

- Session cookies to maintain your login status
- Security cookies to protect user accounts
- Language settings
- Theme preferences

### 2. Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with our platform by collecting anonymized data, such as:

- Pages visited
- Time spent on the platform
- Errors encountered

Currently, we do not use analytics cookies, nor from internal or external service(s)

### 3. Third-Party Cookies (Optional)
